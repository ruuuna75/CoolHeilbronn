Possible Heat Causes

Industrial Areas and Sealed Surfaces

Industrial areas and sealed surfaces store heat during the day and release it in the evening. Our analysis also classifies them as heat-burden zones.

Limited Cooling from Open Land

Open land cools quickly after sunset. But cold air does not reach deep into the city, so the cooling effect is mainly visible beyond ~3 km from the centre.

Limited Tree Canopy Coverage

Trees shade the ground and release moisture from their leaves. Cooling becomes stronger at canopy coverage levels above 40%. Only 1% of the analyzed heated area had more than 25% cover.

Limitations of Inner-City Measurements

The inner city is not the hottest zone. It has the most amount of trees, with about 10% cover, and the bike data stops at 23:00 local time, before a heat island is usually at its strongest.
